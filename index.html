Understanding UPI MDR

MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) is a fee that may be charged to a merchant for accepting certain digital payments. The applicable rate depends on the payment method, merchant arrangement, provider, and applicable rules.

Payment MethodMDR RateNotes
UPIExample / ConfigurableDepends on applicable arrangement
